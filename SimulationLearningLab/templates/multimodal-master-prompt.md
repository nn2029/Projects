# Master Prompt — Multimodal Simulation Learning

Use the `simulation-learning-lab` skill.

Topic or question: [OPTIONAL IF MEDIA MAKES IT CLEAR]
Target learner: [LEVEL / BACKGROUND]
Outcome: By the end, I should be able to [PREDICT / EXPLAIN / DIAGNOSE / CALCULATE].

Inputs may include uploaded images and videos.

For images, inspect the entire asset and preserve provenance for observations. For videos, build a timestamped timeline from representative frames, then inspect denser intervals wherever a meaningful transition is ambiguous. Separate what is directly visible from what you infer.

Do not treat the upload itself as authoritative proof of a general technical claim. Reconcile important factual claims against authoritative external sources.

Build:
1. a multimodal evidence/observation ledger;
2. a causal model;
3. the best simulation grammar for the mechanism;
4. active-learning prediction, trace, failure, retrieval and transfer interactions;
5. an Accuracy & Simplifications panel;
6. the working simulation.

Every media-derived claim must cite the image asset or video timestamp/frame it came from.
